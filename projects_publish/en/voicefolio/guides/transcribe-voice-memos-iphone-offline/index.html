How to transcribe Voice Memos on iPhone offline

A voice memo can be easier to review as text. Import your recording into VoiceFolio, prepare a speech model while connected, then transcribe on your iPhone without sending the recording to a transcription service.

By Bozhu Li · Updated 21 November 2026

Before you start

  • An iPhone with iOS 17 or later and VoiceFolio, plus a recording in Voice Memos.
  • An internet connection for the initial speech-model download, and enough free storage for both the model and recording.
  • Free transcription allowances are limited. Check the current purchase screen for your remaining allowance; Pro expands transcription and saved projects.
01

Share the recording from Voice Memos

Open the recording in Voice Memos and choose Share. Select VoiceFolio in the share sheet to import it. If VoiceFolio is not listed, save the recording to the Files app instead. In VoiceFolio, choose Import from Files and select the saved recording.

Wait for import to finish before starting transcription. Keep your original recording until you have checked the transcript and saved any exports you need.

02

Download a transcription model before going offline

Open VoiceFolio settings and its transcription-model or Model Storage controls. Download the speech model you plan to use. A speech model turns audio into text; it is separate from the optional AI model used for summaries.

Choose a model that suits the recording language and your device. Larger models need more storage and processing resources. Finish the download while online and try a short recording before relying on it away from a connection.

03

Start transcription on your iPhone

Open the imported project, check Source language if you know the spoken language, and choose Start Transcription if transcription has not already started. Once the required model is ready, this audio-to-text step can run offline.

You do not need a cloud AI key for local speech transcription. Cloud summaries are a separate, optional action and need a connection and your own provider key.

04

Check the transcript against the recording

Search for a phrase you need, then tap a timestamped sentence to return to that moment in playback. Correct names, specialist terms and punctuation before sharing the text. Quiet speech, overlapping speakers and background noise can reduce accuracy.

For meeting notes, use the corrected transcript as the record of what was said. If you generate an AI summary or action list, check its wording against that transcript.

05

Save an editable text file

Open Export in the project. Choose TXT for plain text or MD for Markdown, and select Transcript as the content. Enable timestamps if you want time references in a text export, then choose Export & Share and save to Files or another destination.

PDF export requires Pro. If you need timed captions instead of a reading copy, follow the SRT subtitle export guide.

Common questions

VoiceFolio is missing from the share sheet

Save the recording to Files, then use Import from Files inside VoiceFolio. This also gives you a file you can locate again if an import needs to be retried.

The app asks for a download while offline

The selected speech model must be downloaded before offline transcription. Reconnect, complete that model download and retry. Switching models can require a different download.

The text is inaccurate

Check the source language and listen to the affected sentence. Try optional noise reduction or another suitable speech model, then proofread. Transcription accuracy is not guaranteed.
