Good to know

A little context
before you begin.

Local transcription, optional AI providers

Media transcription runs on your device. Local AI models are downloaded separately and depend on device resources; local AI on iPhone is experimental. Cloud AI requires your own API key and internet connection.

Start free, expand with Pro

The free version includes transcription allowances and limited projects. Pro expands transcription, batch operations and projects, and adds features including live captions, speaker separation and PDF export.

Questions, answered

The useful details.

Does VoiceFolio download videos from social platforms?

No. Import files you have saved or downloaded yourself. VoiceFolio analyzes their spoken content through transcription.

Can I transcribe offline?

Yes, once your transcription model is ready. Model downloads require a connection. Optional cloud AI features require an internet connection and your own provider key.

Which export formats are available?

VoiceFolio supports plain text, Markdown, SRT, WebVTT, RTF and PDF. PDF export requires Pro. Bilingual subtitle export is available when a translation exists.

Where does cloud AI send my text?

Only the text you select is sent to your configured provider. You supply the API key, and that provider bills for its usage.
