Getting started

Set up a screen light
for your next shot.

Start with what you have. Make the choices that fit your task.

  1. 01

    Choose a lighting preset

    Open the light panel and choose a look such as Natural Soft or Warm Sun. Swipe left or right on the light to switch presets.

  2. 02

    Adjust and lock the light

    Swipe up or down on the light to adjust screen brightness. Tap to show or hide controls, and touch and hold the light to toggle the control lock.

  3. 03

    Use it as a light or open Camera

    Position the device as a screen light, or open Camera to take a photo or video. Captures go to LumaMe's local library before any optional copy to Photos.

Good to know

Display lighting,
local media and Pro features.

Your originals stay in LumaMe

Media is saved in the private local library first. Copy it to Photos when you choose. Recently Deleted retains items for seven days.

Know what Pro adds

Essential screen light, split lighting, photos and 720p/1080p video are free. Pro adds gradients, more saved presets and supported 4K or higher-frame-rate recording. Display lighting results vary by device and ambient light.

Questions, answered

Screen light and
camera questions.

Can I use LumaMe just as a light?

Yes. Open the screen light without using the camera, including on a second device beside your subject.

Does it change my device's physical color temperature?

The color temperature is simulated by the display. The result depends on your screen and the surrounding light.

Where are my photos and videos saved?

Originals are stored locally in LumaMe. A copy is sent to the system Photos library only when you choose that destination.

Are there ads?

The free version includes ads, which may process device, interaction and diagnostic information. Pro is ad-free. See the privacy policy for details.
