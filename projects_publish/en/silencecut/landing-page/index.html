Getting started

How to remove pauses
with SilenceCut.

Start with what you have. Make the choices that fit your task.

  1. 01

    Import the recording

    In Files, use Select Videos and choose Photo Library or Browse Files. Open the imported recording to see its export settings.

  2. 02

    Choose how to handle pauses

    Expand Detection Parameters. Smart compresses medium pauses and cuts long gaps; Cut removes detected silence. Adjust threshold, minimum duration and padding for the recording.

  3. 03

    Export, play back and save

    Choose Export to process the recording, then play the resulting preview. Return to the settings for another pass if needed, and save or share once the result is right.

Good to know

Local processing,
models and export limits.

Your media stays local

Silence removal processes video and audio on your device. Speech recognition uses on-device models; prepare or download the selected model before using it offline.

Free to try; Pro for more

The free download includes limited exports. Pro adds unlimited exports, watermark-free output and an ad-free experience. Current purchase options are shown in the app.

Questions, answered

Silence removal and
subtitle questions.

Can SilenceCut recognize more than one language?

Yes. Multilingual speech recognition can detect the source language automatically, or you can select it. Examples include English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French and German. Language availability depends on the selected speech model.

Can I review the captions before exporting?

Yes. Enable Edit captions manually and choose Next: Edit Captions. The editor prepares the cut and generates captions, then lets you review words and timing before continuing to export.

Does silence removal require uploading my recording?

No. Silence removal runs on your device. On-device speech recognition needs its model files to be ready; downloading a model requires a connection.

Which devices can I use?

SilenceCut is available for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Check the App Store listing for current compatibility and purchase details.
