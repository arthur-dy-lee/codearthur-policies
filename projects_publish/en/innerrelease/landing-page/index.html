Good to know

A little context
before you begin.

Private records, optional AI

Your records live on-device with optional personal iCloud sync. The optional AI companion uses your own provider key and sends conversations directly to that provider.

Self-help with clear limits

InnerRelease is a self-help and mindfulness tool, not a replacement for counseling, therapy or medical care. Pro expands releases, history, the Deep flow, AI companion use and statistics.

Questions, answered

The useful details.

Do I need an account?

No. Use the app without signing up, and optionally sync records through your personal iCloud.

Is the AI companion required?

No. Guided releases, breathing and SOS tools can be used without it. Cloud AI requires your own supported provider key and an internet connection.

Does it diagnose mental health conditions?

No. Optional questionnaires are educational self-screening tools, not a diagnosis. Seek qualified professional care when needed.

Can I manage my records?

Yes. Review your history and export or delete your data. The app also offers Face ID or Touch ID locking.
