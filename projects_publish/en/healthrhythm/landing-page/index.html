Good to know

A little context
before you begin.

Your records, your permissions

No account is required. Health records stay on-device with optional personal iCloud sync. You choose the Apple Health permissions to grant.

For everyday reflection

HealthRhythm is a daily tracking tool, not a diagnosis. Food estimates and observed patterns need your judgment. Pro adds longer trends, advanced correlations, unlimited custom charts and widgets.

Questions, answered

The useful details.

Do I need to enter all my health data manually?

No. With your permission, HealthRhythm reads supported data from Apple Health. You add information such as meal, medication and gut logs.

Can I choose which metrics appear together?

Yes. Custom charts let you select metrics and compare them on a shared timeline. Unlimited custom charts are part of Pro.

Does a pattern prove that one habit caused a change?

No. The app helps you review records and trends. A correlation is not a medical diagnosis or proof of cause.

Do I need an account?

No account or login is required. Optional sync uses your personal iCloud.
