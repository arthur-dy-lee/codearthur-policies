Good to know

On-device speech,
recording and Pro limits.

Speech stays on-device

Use built-in offline Whisper or Apple on-device recognition. Available languages and response depend on the engine, model and device. Manual and fixed-speed prompting do not need speech recognition.

Free practice, optional Pro

Manual prompting and script creation remain unlimited. Try voice follow and recording with free allowances; Pro removes ads and unlocks unlimited use. Camera features described here are for iPhone and iPad.

Questions, answered

Voice-follow teleprompter
questions.

What happens when I pause or go off script?

Voice Follow tracks your reading progress rather than a timer. It holds your place when you pause and offers recovery controls when you return after an ad-lib.

Can I use it without speech recognition?

Yes. Fixed Speed and Manual modes do not require speech recognition.

Does PromptTempo add a watermark?

No. Recordings and exports are watermark-free.

Can I export subtitles?

Voice Follow recordings can export script-based SRT subtitles when reliable timing is available. This uses the script and recording timing.
