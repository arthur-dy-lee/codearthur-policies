Questions, answered

Budget and FIRE
planning questions.

Is this only a retirement calculator?

No. FinanceFreedom also tracks everyday income, expenses, budgets, assets and liabilities, so your FIRE plan sits alongside your actual financial records.

Can I compare retirement approaches?

Yes. Compare Standard, Lean, Fat, Barista and Coast FIRE, including semi-retirement and full retirement milestones.

Are projected retirement dates guaranteed?

No. Projections and simulations are estimates based on the data and assumptions you provide. They do not guarantee retirement dates or investment returns.

Does voice entry save a transaction immediately?

You can review and edit the transcript, amount, date and category before saving. Cloud AI requires your configured service and consent.
