Good to know

Comfort, safety
and library access.

A library with boundaries

Some topics are information-only or require professional guidance. BreathLever is a general-wellness and educational tool for adults, not a medical device.

Comfort comes first

Do not force breathing or holds; stop if you feel discomfort. Never practice while driving, in water or operating machinery. Pro expands the guided library, course and customization and removes ads.

Questions, answered

Guided breathing
and practice questions.

Are all 32 topics guided exercises?

No. There are 21 guided practices plus 11 safety and education entries. Some methods are information-only or require professional guidance.

Can I stop or skip a hold?

Yes. Pause or stop any time, and skip an optional hold. Keep the practice comfortable rather than forcing a pattern.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

BreathLever includes an Apple Watch experience with wrist haptics, alongside its iPhone and iPad app.

Do I need to sign up?

No account is required. Practice history, check-ins and journal entries stay on your device by default.
