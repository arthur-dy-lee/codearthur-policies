# App page SEO and practical guides — 2026-11-04

This release improves the eight existing English product pages and adds three focused guides plus a guide index. All product URLs, the current SilenceCut icon, Google verification, and existing privacy and Pro qualifications are preserved.

## Search intent and page scope

These are editorial search-intent candidates based on the actual app features. They are not measured keyword volumes, ranking predictions or guaranteed traffic opportunities.

| App | Primary candidate intent | Important qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SilenceCut | Remove pauses from iPhone video; recognize and proofread multilingual captions | Detection runs during export or preparation for manual caption review; original-language recognition is distinct from translation. |
| FinanceFreedom | Budgeting, net worth and FIRE planning | Projections and simulated results depend on inputs and assumptions; no guaranteed returns or retirement date. |
| VoiceFolio | On-device audio transcription and searchable meeting notes | Prepare local models first; optional cloud AI requires the user's own provider key and connection. |
| PromptTempo | Voice-follow teleprompter and recording on iPhone/iPad | Voice Follow, Fixed Speed and Manual are distinct choices; recording details are device dependent. |
| LumaMe | Use iPhone/iPad as a screen fill light | Screen characteristics and ambient light affect results; gradients and some camera options require Pro. |
| HealthRhythm | Daily health log with sleep, meals and other records | Logging and comparisons do not diagnose conditions or establish causality. |
| BreathLever | Guided breathing library and practice timer | General wellness, with method-specific safety guidance and the ability to stop. |
| InnerRelease | Guided reflection and private emotional check-ins | Optional AI uses the user's own provider key; self-reflection is not therapy. |

Every product page now has a distinct descriptive title and H1, task-specific section headings, a concise three-step workflow, and screenshot descriptions matched to what the official images actually show. Hero screenshot alt text is updated as well as the screenshot gallery.
