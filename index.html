Explore tools for video, voice, scripts, photography, personal finance and daily wellbeing. Each app below links to its official App Store page.

SilenceCut — Remove Silence

Automatically remove silent pauses from video and audio recordings. Adjust the volume threshold, minimum silence duration and padding, then preview the result before exporting. Useful for spoken videos, podcasts, interviews and lessons.

Record or import audio and video, transcribe speech on your device and keep a searchable, timestamped transcript. Turn meeting, lecture and interview transcripts into summaries, action items or mind maps with configurable AI options.

PromptTempo — Teleprompter
